# Textback - Go-To-Market Plan (who to sell to and how)

Plain-English strategy for selling Textback. Evidence-backed. Written after real feedback from a working realtor.

## The pivot: sell to trades, not realtors

Real feedback (realtor, ~2,000 sales/month county): realtors do the **chasing** themselves. Nobody cold-calls a realtor, so there are almost no missed inbound calls to catch, and in a competitive market the review-chasing upside is thin. He pointed at **laborers and service trades** instead. The data agrees: trades live and die on **inbound** calls they miss while on the job. So Textback's target is **home-service trades**, and the hero feature is **missed-call text-back**.

## Why trades are the right buyer (the numbers)

- Trades miss **20-30% of calls** (seasonal peaks 40-50%) because they are on job sites.
- Each missed call is worth **$275-$1,200**; HVAC averages **~$1,200**, and high-ticket jobs (HVAC replace, roofing, repipe) are **$3,000-$15,000**.
- A shop missing 5-10 calls/week loses **$45,000-$120,000+ a year** (mid-size HVAC up to $252k).
- Speed matters: after 5 minutes, conversion drops **80%**; after 30 minutes the lead is basically gone.

That is the entire sales pitch: **you are already losing tens of thousands a year to missed calls; Textback texts those callers back in 10 seconds so they do not call the next guy.**

## Best verticals, ranked for a solo starter

Pick ONE to start. Best = high missed-call pain + high job value (huge ROI story) + urgent inbound calls + easy to find locally.

| Rank | Trade | Why it fits | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HVAC** | Emergency calls, ~$1,200/call avg, $3k-15k replacements, misses calls in heat/cold peaks | Highest ROI story; spends on marketing |
| 2 | **Plumbing** | Emergencies, frequent inbound, loses up to $234k/yr to missed calls | Huge, easy to find, urgent |
| 3 | **Electrical** | Urgent inbound, high ticket | Similar to plumbing |
| 4 | **Garage door / water damage & restoration** | Emergency, high ticket, "call the first to answer" | Very strong missed-call fit |
| 5 | **Roofing / doors & windows** | Highest willingness to pay (cost-per-lead $228 / $200) | Bigger ticket but more outbound/storm-driven, longer sales cycle |

Willingness-to-pay signal (what they already pay per lead): roofing $228, doors/windows $200, construction $165, HVAC $85, plumbing $76. Trades spend **8-12% of revenue** on marketing, so a $49/mo tool that recovers one job is a rounding error to them.

**Recommendation:** start with **HVAC or plumbing** in your local area. Emergency inbound + big ticket = the missed-call pitch lands hardest, and there are thousands of them.

## Use your warm connection

Your brother-in-law is a realtor, which is perfect: realtors constantly **refer and work with trades** (plumbers, HVAC, electricians, handymen, restoration). Ask him for 2-3 intros to trades he trusts. A warm intro beats 50 cold calls. First customers from a referral close far easier.

## The best way to reach them (evidence on channel)

Comparing outreach channels for reaching busy business owners:

| Channel | Reply / success | Speed | Best used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SMS** | ~**45% reply** (vs 6% email); 98% open; read in 5 min | Fastest | **Follow-up after opt-in**, NOT cold outreach (B2B needs consent; cold texts get filtered/are risky) |
| **Phone call** | ~2-6.7% to a meeting; **82% of buyers** accept meetings from strategic calls; takes ~6 calls to connect | Immediate if answered | First contact + booking a demo |
| **In-person (door-knock)** | High for hyper-local; drop a one-pager, ask for 10 min | n/a | Local trade shops; feels old-school, works |
| **Email** | 3-5% reply (top 15-25%); 20% open | Slow | Supporting info + follow-up in a sequence |

**Takeaway:** for cold outreach to trades, **phone + in-person win**, backed by short email. **SMS is for follow-up once they have opted in** (do not cold-text businesses; carriers filter it and it needs consent). Multi-channel beats any single channel: call, then email, then (after opt-in) text.

Rules that matter for trades specifically:
- Talk in **trade language**, not corporate speak. Trades spot fake personalization in 3 seconds; be operationally specific ("you miss calls on the tools, that is money out the door").
- Lead with **money** (jobs won/lost on response time), not features.

## The pitch (say it like this)

> "You are on the tools all day, so you miss maybe a quarter of your calls. Each missed call is a few hundred to over a thousand bucks in work. That is tens of grand a year walking to the next guy who picks up. Textback texts every missed caller back in 10 seconds, as if from you, so they stay yours. It is $49 a month. Recover one job and it has paid for itself for the year. Want me to switch it on free for a month so you can see it catch a call?"

Killer demo: **do the missed-call stunt on them** (like your brother-in-law demo). Call them, they miss it or you miss theirs, then show the instant text-back. They feel it.

## How to get the first 10 customers (playbook)

1. **Pick one trade + your town** (e.g. "HVAC in [your city]"). Niche beats generic.
2. **Warm intros first** - brother-in-law + your network - 2-3 names. Start there.
3. **Build a target list** of 30-50 local shops (Google Maps, "HVAC near me"). Note the ones with **bad/few reviews or that did not answer when you called** - they need this most.
4. **Reach out, phone-first:** call the owner, use the money pitch, offer a **free month + white-glove setup**. If no answer, short email/voicemail, then follow up. Persistence: it takes ~6 touches.
5. **Door-knock a few shops** with a one-page "you are losing $X/year to missed calls" leave-behind.
6. **Be useful in local Facebook groups** (trade + community buy/sell) and a **trade association / Chamber of Commerce** (PHCC, ACCA, NECA). Answer questions, do not spam.
7. **First 10 = free design partners:** free setup + the money-back guarantee, in exchange for honest feedback + a testimonial + referrals. Trades refer heavily to each other.
8. **Turn results into proof:** the moment Textback catches someone a real job, get the quote/screenshot. That single story sells the next 10.

Realistic math: founder-led outbound gets customer-acquisition cost around **$200-800** and a few dozen calls should land your first handful. This is offline, unsexy sales - that is exactly why it works and why big software ignores it.

## What to avoid

- Do not go broad ("any business"). One trade, one town, then expand.
- Do not cold-text businesses (compliance + filtering). SMS is for after they say yes.
- Do not pitch features. Pitch **money recovered**.
- Do not skip the warm intros to grind cold - use the realtor connection.
